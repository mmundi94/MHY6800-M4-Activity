Team facts

Vela playbook fictional

Vela, its people, and its numbers are fictional and were written for this practice. People are described by role only.

The team

WhoWhat to know
Product managerOrders the work by value and can make the call alone.
Team leadLikes coaching and removing obstacles.
3 developersBuild the app and the bakery screens.
TesterTests each new piece of work during the Sprint.
DesignerDesigns the screens.
Two pilot bakery ownersStakeholders. Can attend one meeting per Sprint.
FounderStakeholder. Wants to see working results at the end of each Sprint.

Sprint facts

  • Two weeks (10 working days), starting on a Monday.
  • Capacity: about 20 story points. A story point is a relative size.
  • Product Goal: customers can order a custom cake from a local bakery on any phone, without calling.

Team problems

  • Only one developer understands the payment code.
  • Bugs fixed last month keep coming back, and the team is afraid to change the order code.
  • Merging everyone's work at the end of the Sprint takes two days and breaks other features.
  • Bakeries see new features only every three months, so feedback comes too late.
